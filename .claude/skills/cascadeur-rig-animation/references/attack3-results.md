## Method (scripts attack3_base.py, keypolish.py, grid9_chunk.py, dp_assemble.py)
1. Design keys in the torso frame (`A3_KEYS`: hips, pelvis, torso, head, grip position, blade direction in WORLD coordinates, heel lift, feet) with Hermite interpolation (`hermite_keys`, zero tangents at the reversal and hold keys, start tangent = Attack 2's end velocity).
2. The designed keys were NOT anatomically feasible (wrist ulnar 47-77, left forearm 1 cm from the torso, twist 95). A random search per key (`keypolish.kp_polish`) found cost-3 poses, but three traps: (a) with the blade direction left free it simply dropped the point 25-35 degrees off the aim line, so keep the direction nudges at 0 (torso extras compensate and hold the world pose); (b) polishing keys independently lands them on different swivel branches (right swivel -40 at one key, +80 at the next) and the in-between frames violate limits, so chain the keys in time order with a continuity term on swivels and extras; (c) the optimiser prefers a chamber that is not drawn in, so give the chamber keys a `zmax` limit on the grip's forward distance from Spine2 (30, 26, 16, 10 were too tight and forced torso roll 20 / pelvis pitch 15; 36, 27, 22 was the compromise: cost 25 at the chamber).
3. Rebuild the base through the polished key vectors (`apply_off7(base, x)`), grid + DP over roll/swivel with the lead rule off (a thrust has no edge to lead with; `W_TRUE = W_EITHER = 0`), twist limits 45/80.
