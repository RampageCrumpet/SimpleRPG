# Attack 1 (right diagonal) baseline and history

Project `I:/Art/Cascadeur/Longsword_Attack1_RightDiagonal.casc` (backup `..._backup_0131.casc`, previews in `I:/Art/Cascadeur/Previews/`). Neutral clip frames 0-130, Combo clip frames 140-227 (combo frame f at 140+f). 60 fps; frame 0 is the start pose.

Timings used (frames): windup 0-56, hit 56-70, combo recovery 70-87, neutral finish 70-130. Chamber peak about frame 52, blade peak speed 25 m/s at frame 59, overswing peak (Neutral) frame 84 (grip 59 cm from Ready), rebound about frame 110 (tip 8 cm above Ready), settle to exact Ready at 130.

Design (torso-frame keys in `scripts/attack1_v3.py`): charge leans back and toward the right (counter to the left-down swing): about -12 pitch and +15 roll at frame 52, hips shifted to the left; cut folds forward and to the left (torso yaw -38 at chamber to +46 at overswing).

Last measured values on the evaluated scene (before the anatomy and clearance review): wrist swing 84/82 (R/L), edge within 18 degrees of travel through frame 70, feet fixed (left heel lift 2.3 cm), head within a few degrees of forward. Problems found afterwards: ulnar deviation up to 82 degrees, left elbow inside the torso at frames 72-84, hand carrying up to 75 degrees of pronation because the rig cannot twist the forearm.

Mistakes worth not repeating: measuring wrists from the solver instead of the scene; treating the combined swing angle as one limit; random-search optimisation leaving frame-to-frame twist noise (smooth the offsets); adding an edge-leading requirement without a body-clearance term; rebuilding the project from a stale file and losing the playback range; sending user an 'edge leads' claim measured loosely instead of against the actual tip travel direction.

## v9 (clean tip path) - 2026-10-15
User report: the tip from frames 60-85 wobbled instead of tracing one clean swing; also suggested raising the elbow to stop the arm swinging flat.
Cause of the wobble: the v7/v8 time warp (`warp7`) linearly interpolated between per-frame vectors, so tip speed jumped 13 -> 24 -> 14 -> 27 cm/frame over frames 64-71 and the tip changed direction by 30-80 degrees per frame at frames 66-68 and 79-81. The offsets were not the cause (final tip path equals the base tip path).
Fix (`scripts/retime9.py`): Gaussian-smooth the pose columns (sigma 2 frames, ramps at both ends), then re-time along the smoothed tip path with a monotone speed decay that starts at the real speed at the peak and ends at 0 (Neutral) or at the old end speed (Combo, `floor`), sampling vectors with Catmull-Rom instead of lerp. Recovery 88-126 smoothed the same way (sigma 2). Roll/swivel re-solved with `gridopt9.py` + `grid9_chunk.py` (roll offsets -100..100 step 25, swivel step 24, DP, then [1 2 1] smoothing of the roll/swivel columns because the 24-degree grid steps otherwise show up as elbow snaps).
Scene results: tip speed now falls smoothly 22.6 m/s (frame 58) to 0 (frame 87), largest direction change 17.6 deg/frame (was 82), zero changes above 25 (was 5-6). Right wrist flex 65, ext 31, radial 19, ulnar 31; left flex 37, ext 35, radial 14, ulnar 33; left twist [-94, 43] (Neutral), [-85, 31] (Combo); left elbow clearance 21.2 (Neutral) / 22.4 (Combo).
Elbow-up experiments (frames 64-80): the left elbow hangs 14-26 cm below the shoulder. Scanning the left swivel at a fixed sword pose: raising the elbow either raises forearm twist (swivel toward -180) or collapses elbow clearance / drives ulnar deviation past 50 (swivel toward +60). Rolling the sword 25-50 deg to put the edge forward needs left twist -86 to -108. Raising or lowering the sword position by 8 cm, or moving it 8 cm along torso x/z, changed left twist by under 10 degrees. An elbow-up cost term (`elbow_up_cost`, weight 3-6) and an edge weight x12 did not remove flat-leading in 69-77 without exceeding hard limits. Flat-leading frames in v9: 69-77 (angle to edge 37-71 deg, 11 -> 3.5 m/s). So the remaining lever is elsewhere (torso/shoulder orientation or the sword's blade direction), not the swivel.
